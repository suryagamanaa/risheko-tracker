# RiSHEko Tracker

An interactive questionnaire that maps a woman's financial condition and risk, then gives personalized recommendations for action.

**Live demo:** https://risheko-tracker.vercel.app

RiSHEko Tracker is one of three tools in **SHE-UP! (Sinergi Hidup Ekonomi Perempuan)**, a cross-generational financial literacy program by team TRIHAS (IPB University), supported by Astra through the ACTION! program.

## Why this exists

Many women already know financial concepts but still struggle to act on them. Our field survey showed the gap:

- Ibu-ibu (n=60): 45.8% do not yet understand the risk of quick loans, and 45.0% have not prioritized an emergency fund.
- Mahasiswi (n=40): 52.5% do not regularly record their finances, and 42.5% have not set aside an emergency fund.

RiSHEko Tracker turns this gap into something a user can see for herself, then points to the next step.

## What it does

- Two user profiles: **Momprenuer** and **Woman Youth**.
- Questionnaire built on the Knowledge-Attitude-Practice (KAP) approach, with 9 core questions covering emergency funds, record keeping, and debt risk, scored 0 to 2.
- Shows a financial risk picture, followed by personalized action recommendations.
- Used as a pre-test and post-test at program events, opened through a QR code.
- Interactive, super-app-inspired interface designed for use on a phone.

## Results from the program

The tracker was used as the pre/post instrument at two events:

| Event | Participants | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ruang Temu Bunda SHE-Aga (Desa Cibatok and Desa Kalong II) | 88 ibu-ibu | 98% aware of the importance of insurance; 76.9% good at recording finances |
| SHE-UP! Campus Fest 2026 (IPB University) | 102 mahasiswi | 99% aware of insurance; financial literacy score up 16% (76.8 to 89.7) |
